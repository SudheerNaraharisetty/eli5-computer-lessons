Meet Maya again. Her cookie shop has a receipt printer, the X two hundred.

Three parts of her program use it. The receipt, the kitchen ticket, and the loyalty coupon.

Here is the code. Look closely. Every part reaches right inside the printer. Into its memory, and into its motor. Six lines that know this printer's insides.

Then the printer breaks. Maya buys a different brand. Inside, nothing has the same name. So every line snaps. One. Two. Three. Four. Five. Six. All she did was swap a printer.

The fix is one small job, called print line. Only it knows the printer's insides. Every other part just asks it to print.

Next time the printer changes, Maya fixes one place. The other parts never notice.

That is loose coupling. Parts that know as little as possible about each other.
