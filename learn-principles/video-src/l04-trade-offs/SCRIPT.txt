Maya learned a good trick. Give a value one name, in one place. Now she uses it everywhere.

Here is a tiny job. It prints the total, then Thank you. Three lines.

Maya gives the words a name. Then the exclamation mark. Then a switch to turn it off. Then a settings file, and a helper for each. Three lines are now nineteen, in four files.

Then a simple request. Change the message. Where is it? The shop file. Then greeting. Then helpers. Then settings. Four files, for two words.

Same goal. Opposite mistake. Too little structure hurts. Too much hurts too. Aim for just enough.

Before you use any principle, ask two questions. Is this problem real right now? What will this cost the next reader?

Principles are trade-offs, not laws.
