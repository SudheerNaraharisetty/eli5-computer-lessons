Maya's cookie shop now sells online too.

So the price of a cookie is written down in three places. The shop menu. The website. And the loyalty app. Three dollars, three times.

Then Maya raises the price to four dollars. She changes the menu. But the website and the app still say three.

A customer orders online for three dollars. At pickup, the shop charges four. Two answers to one question, and nobody can say which is right.

The fix. Pick one owner. The menu owns the price. The website and the app stop keeping their own copy. They read it from the menu.

Next time the price changes, Maya changes one place, and every screen follows.

You saw this in lesson one, with the tax rate. Now it has a name.

Single source of truth. Every fact lives in one place.
