Maya's shop has a job called get total. What would you guess it does? Anyone would say, it just gives back a number.

But look inside. Line three. It also charges the customer's card. Nothing in the name says so.

Sam, Maya's new helper, is asked to show the total on the customer's screen. He reads the name, and calls get total.

A customer orders ten dollars of cookies. The screen shows ten ninety. And the card is charged. Then comes the checkout. It calls get total again. The card is charged a second time.

Sam did nothing silly. The name made a promise, and the code broke it.

The fix. Get total only works out the total. The charging moves to the checkout, in plain sight.

Now the screen can show the total as often as it likes. One order, one charge.

That is least astonishment. Code should do what its name says, and nothing more.
