Maya's loyalty card has one shop rule. Points can never go below zero.

In her code, the points sit like a jar on an open shelf. Four jobs reach in and change the number themselves.

Then a fifth job arrives. Refunds. It takes twenty points back, and it never checks.

Ben had eight points. Now he has minus twelve. Days later, Maya sees it on a receipt. Five jobs touch that jar. Which one did it?

The fix. Move the points behind a counter, with two small doors. Earn, and spend. Nothing else can touch them.

Spend checks first. Not enough points? It says no.

Now run the refund again. Twenty from eight? Refused. Ben keeps his eight points. And the rule lives in one place.

That is encapsulation. Keep the insides private, and let the doors check the rules.
