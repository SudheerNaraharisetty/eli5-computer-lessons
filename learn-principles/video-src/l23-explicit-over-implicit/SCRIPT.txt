Tuesday is sale day at Maya's cookie shop. Ten percent off.

Sam writes the job that works out the price. Picture a machine with one slot on the front. A price goes in. A price comes out.

On Tuesday, Sam tries it. Three dollars in. Two seventy out.

On Monday night, he prints the tags for tomorrow's sale. Three dollars in. Three dollars out. Same input, different answer.

Look behind the machine. A hidden pipe runs in from the computer's clock. The job quietly asks what day it is today.

The fix: bring that pipe round to the front. Now the job takes the price, and the day. Sam asks for Tuesday, and gets two seventy on any day.

Say what your code depends on, right where it is used. Explicit is better than implicit.
