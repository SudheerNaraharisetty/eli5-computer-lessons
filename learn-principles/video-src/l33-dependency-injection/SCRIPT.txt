Maya's checkout needs four helpers. A card machine. A sales book. A printer. And the clock.

Today it goes and gets them itself. Four arms reach under the counter, where nobody looks. From the front, it seems to need only a price.

So to try it out, Maya has to charge a real card, write in the real sales book, and print real paper. She buys a cookie from herself, for one cent. One day, that cent lands in her tax report.

The fix. Pull the arms in. Cut four slots in the front. Now one small file, the wiring, hands checkout its parts.

For the shop, the real ones. For a try-out, pretend ones. Nothing real is touched. For shop two, a different printer. Lines changed in checkout: zero.

That is dependency injection. Do not let code fetch its own parts. Hand it what it needs.
