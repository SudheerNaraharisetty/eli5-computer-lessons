Maya wrote a small job called box price. It prices a box of cookies. Sam's party screen calls it.

Maya knows a box holds one to one hundred cookies. But that rule lives only in her head.

Then a wedding asks for two hundred and fifty. Back comes one hundred and twenty five dollars. The price of a box of fifty.

Sam says, your function is wrong. Maya says, you were never meant to send that. So whose bug is it? Nobody can say.

So they write the deal down. The caller promises: a count from one to one hundred. The code promises: a price above zero. And a check, called an assert, guards each promise.

Play it again. Two hundred and fifty goes in. It stops at once. The message says whose side broke the deal. The caller's. The wrong price never appears.

That is design by contract. Write down what the code needs, and what it promises back.
