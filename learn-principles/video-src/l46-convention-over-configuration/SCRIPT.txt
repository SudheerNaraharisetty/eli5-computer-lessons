Maya's shop now sells twelve things. Each one needs a page, a photo, and a label.

So her settings file spells out every one. Twelve products, three settings each. Thirty-six lines.

Look closely. Almost every line just repeats the product's name. Only two are really different.

Then Sam adds ginger snaps. Three more lines. He gets one right, mistypes one, and forgets the third. Now the new page shows a broken picture.

The fix is one house rule, written once. A product's page, photo and label all follow from its name.

Thirty-four lines disappear. The settings file keeps only the two real exceptions, in plain sight.

Now ginger snaps need just a name. The page, the photo and the label fall into place.

Agree on the usual way. Write it down. Spell out only what is different. That is convention over configuration.
