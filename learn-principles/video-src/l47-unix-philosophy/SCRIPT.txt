Maya asks one question. What were today's three best sellers?

Sam starts building one big program to answer it. Three weeks later it has forty options. Then Maya asks, how many ginger? There is no button for that.

The accountant grew up with Unix. She builds nothing. She lines up four small tools, joined by pipes.

In goes the pile of sales. Sort puts them in order. Count squashes each bunch into one line with a number. Rank puts the biggest first. And top three keeps three. There is Maya's answer.

Each tool does one job. Each one hands plain lines of text to the next, through the same simple pipe.

So for the ginger question, she just unclips them. Find ginger. Count the lines. Two. Nothing new was built.

That is the Unix philosophy. Small tools that do one thing well, made to be combined.
