One cracked window, left alone, tells the whole street that nobody cares. Soon there are more. Code has broken windows too.

This is the test board for Maya's shop. Forty-eight lights, one for each test. One has been red for a month. Everyone says, ignore that one.

Monday. Priya is new, and in a hurry. She sees the red light, the old notes, the screen of warnings. So she skips her own test, and leaves a note too. Nobody notices.

Three months later, six lights are red. Then a seventh turns red. A real bug. But red is normal here, so it hides for two weeks.

Now play it again, on a clean board. Forty-eight green. Same Priya, same hurry. Her change turns one light red. It is the only red on the wall. She fixes it before lunch.

Same person. Different surroundings. Mess does not come from bad people. It comes from what looks normal. So don't live with broken windows.
