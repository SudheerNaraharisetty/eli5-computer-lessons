Ana, Ben and Chloe are coming to dinner, and dinner needs a little maths. Python does it with signs you already know.

Double the pasta. Two hundred and fifty, times two. Five hundred grams.

Now split the bill. Dinner is sixty dollars, plus a fifteen dollar tip, shared by three friends.

Sixty plus fifteen, divided by three. Python says sixty five each. That is wrong.

Python divides before it adds. So only the tip got split.

Put brackets around the sum. Brackets go first. Now each friend pays twenty five.

See the point zero? One slash always gives a float, even when it divides evenly.

Now dessert. Fourteen cookies, three plates. Two slashes deal whole cookies only. Four each.

The percent sign gives the leftovers. Two cookies, still on the tray.

Brackets first. One slash for exact. Two slashes for whole. Percent for the rest.
