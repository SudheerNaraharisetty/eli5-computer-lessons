Ana ran every day this week. Seven numbers on one list. Picture it as a loaf of bread, with seven slices, numbered zero to six.

You want her first three days. Write the list name, then square brackets with zero, colon, three. That is a slice.

Zero is where the knife starts. Three is where it stops. But slice three stays on the loaf. Just like range, the stop is not included.

The knife lifts away a new list: slices zero, one and two. Five, eight and six kilometres. And the loaf is still whole. The original list does not change.

Leave a number out, and the cut runs to the end. Minus three, colon, gives the last three days.

A second colon adds a step. Step two takes every second slice. Zero, two, four and six.

Step minus one flips the loaf on the board. Sunday comes first.

Start, stop, step. The stop is never included, and the loaf stays whole.
