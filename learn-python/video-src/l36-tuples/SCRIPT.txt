This is grandma's pancake card. Two hundred grams of flour, three hundred millilitres of milk, two eggs. The ratio never changes.

Keep it on a scrap of paper, as a list in square brackets, and anyone can scribble on it. One line doubles the flour for Ben. And every batch after that is wrong.

So laminate the card. Round brackets make a tuple. A tuple is a list you cannot change.

You can still read it. Index zero is the flour. A slice gives flour and milk.

But try to write on it. Pancake, zero, equals four hundred. Python refuses. Type error. A tuple does not support item assignment. Grandma's ratio is safe.

Need a double batch? Unpack the card into three jars: flour, milk and eggs. Then make new values. The card stays the same.

One trap. Egg in round brackets is just text. Add a comma, and now it is a tuple.

Use a tuple for things that must not change.
