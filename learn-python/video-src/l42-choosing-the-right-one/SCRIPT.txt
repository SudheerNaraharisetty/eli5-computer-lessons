Kitchen Coach is planning a dinner party. It has four jobs, and four drawers: a list, a tuple, a dict and a set.

Job one: the order of the courses. Does the order matter, and will it change? Yes and yes. That is a list.

Job two: the oven setting. One hundred and eighty degrees, fan. A fixed record. That is a tuple.

Job three: each guest's protein goal. Will you look it up by a name? Then use a dict.

Job four: every allergen anyone has. You only ask, is it in there? That is a set.

Pick the wrong drawer. Put the guests in a list, and finding Ben means walking past every item, one by one. Put the allergens in a list, and nuts shows up twice.

Swap in a dict, and Python jumps straight to Ben. Swap in a set, and nuts appears once. The code gets shorter, too.

Pick the container by the question you will ask it.
