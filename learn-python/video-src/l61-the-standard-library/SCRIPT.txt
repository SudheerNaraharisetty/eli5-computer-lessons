Kitchen Coach needs a few everyday tools. Pick a dinner for Friday. Count the days to Ana's race. Count this week's meals. So you roll up your sleeves and write them yourself.

Take the race. Ana runs on the twenty-eighth of February. Today is the twenty-third of November. Your counter treats every month as thirty days. It says ninety-five.

But December and January have thirty-one days. The real answer is ninety-seven. Ana would start her taper two days late.

Python comes with a shelf of free cookbooks. Random. Date time. Collections. Math. Statistics. People call it batteries included.

From date time, import date. Race day minus today. Ninety-seven days, every month counted right.

Counter tallies the week's meals in one line. Oats, three times. And random choice picks Friday's dinner.

To find a tool, search the Python docs, or ask help.

Before you write it, check the free cookbooks. Python probably has it.
