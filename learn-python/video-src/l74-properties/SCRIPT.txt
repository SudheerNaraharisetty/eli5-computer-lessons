Chloe's gluten-free brownies. Twelve of them. Kitchen Coach keeps the recipe in a jar, with a label on the front. Total, two thousand four hundred calories.

Who wrote that label? Init did, once. Self dot total K cal equals the sum.

Now add walnuts. Two hundred and forty more calories. The real total is two thousand six hundred and forty. The label still says two thousand four hundred. It is stale.

The fix is a property. At property, on top of a method. Now the label is worked out every time you read it. No brackets.

Read it again. Two thousand six hundred and forty. And each brownie, two hundred and twenty.

A property can guard a value, too. Add a setter. It runs every time you assign. Someone types servings equals zero. That would divide by zero. The guard says no. Value error. Servings must be at least one.

A property looks like an attribute, but runs code. The label is always right.
