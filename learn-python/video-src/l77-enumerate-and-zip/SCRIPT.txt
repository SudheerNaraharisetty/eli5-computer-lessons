Chloe's gluten-free pancakes. Four steps on the recipe card. Kitchen Coach prints them with a loop over range of len. Step zero? Nobody cooks step zero. Every number is one too low.

Python has a tool for this. Enumerate. It hands the loop two things at once: a number, and the step. Say start equals one, and the steps are numbered one, two, three, four.
