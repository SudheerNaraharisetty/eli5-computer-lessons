Every morning, Kitchen Coach checks twenty thousand orders against the pantry. Four stations. Read the orders, check the pantry, cook, plate.

The stopwatch says two seconds. But which station is slow? Do not guess. Measure.

Run it under the profiler. It puts a stopwatch on every function. Read orders, almost nothing. Cook and plate, almost nothing. Check pantry, ninety nine percent of the time.

Zoom in. The pantry is a list, one long shelf. To answer "is it here?", the clerk walks the shelf, item by item. For twenty thousand orders, that is four hundred fifty seven million looks.

A set is a wall of labelled drawers. Python goes straight to the right drawer and opens it. One look.

Change one line. The pantry becomes a set. Profile again. The bar shrinks to nothing, and the stopwatch drops from two seconds to one hundredth of a second.

Measure first. The profiler shows the slow step. Fix that one.
